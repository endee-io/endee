Interstellar (2014) - Directed by Christopher Nolan. A team of explorers travel through a wormhole in space in an attempt to ensure humanity's survival. The film explores themes of love, time, gravity, and the survival of the human race. Stars Matthew McConaughey, Anne Hathaway, and Jessica Chastain. Genre: Sci-Fi, Drama, Adventure.

Blade Runner 2049 (2017) - Directed by Denis Villeneuve. A young blade runner's discovery of a long-buried secret leads him to track down former blade runner Rick Deckard, who's been missing for thirty years. The film explores themes of identity, memory, and what it means to be human. Stars Ryan Gosling and Harrison Ford. Genre: Sci-Fi, Drama, Thriller.

The Matrix (1999) - Directed by the Wachowskis. A computer programmer discovers that reality as he knows it is a simulation created by machines, and joins a rebellion to overthrow them. Revolutionary visual effects and deep philosophical themes about free will and the nature of reality. Stars Keanu Reeves. Genre: Sci-Fi, Action.

Arrival (2016) - Directed by Denis Villeneuve. When mysterious spacecraft touch down across the globe, a linguist is recruited by the military to help communicate with the alien visitors. The film beautifully explores language, time, and the choices we make. Stars Amy Adams and Jeremy Renner. Genre: Sci-Fi, Drama.

Dune (2021) - Directed by Denis Villeneuve. A brilliant and gifted young man born into a great destiny beyond his understanding must travel to the most dangerous planet in the universe to ensure the future of his family and his people. Based on Frank Herbert's novel. Stars Timothée Chalamet. Genre: Sci-Fi, Adventure, Drama.

2001: A Space Odyssey (1968) - Directed by Stanley Kubrick. After discovering a mysterious artifact buried beneath the lunar surface, mankind sets off on a quest to find its origins with the help of intelligent supercomputer HAL 9000. A groundbreaking masterpiece of cinema. Genre: Sci-Fi, Mystery.

Ex Machina (2014) - Directed by Alex Garland. A young programmer is selected to participate in a groundbreaking experiment in synthetic intelligence by evaluating the human qualities of a highly advanced humanoid A.I. Explores themes of consciousness, manipulation, and the ethics of artificial intelligence. Stars Domhnall Gleeson, Alicia Vikander, and Oscar Isaac. Genre: Sci-Fi, Thriller, Drama.

The Martian (2015) - Directed by Ridley Scott. An astronaut becomes stranded on Mars after his team assume him dead, and must rely on his ingenuity to find a way to signal to Earth that he is alive. Based on Andy Weir's novel. Stars Matt Damon. Genre: Sci-Fi, Adventure, Drama.

Inception (2010) - Directed by Christopher Nolan. A thief who steals corporate secrets through the use of dream-sharing technology is given the inverse task of planting an idea into the mind of a C.E.O. Mind-bending exploration of dreams within dreams. Stars Leonardo DiCaprio. Genre: Sci-Fi, Action, Thriller.

Gravity (2013) - Directed by Alfonso Cuarón. Two astronauts work together to survive after an accident leaves them stranded in space. A visually stunning survival thriller that captures the terrifying beauty and danger of outer space. Stars Sandra Bullock and George Clooney. Genre: Sci-Fi, Thriller, Drama.

Annihilation (2018) - Directed by Alex Garland. A biologist signs up for a dangerous, secret expedition into a mysterious zone where the laws of nature don't apply. The film explores themes of self-destruction, identity, and the unknown. Stars Natalie Portman. Genre: Sci-Fi, Horror, Mystery.

Her (2013) - Directed by Spike Jonze. In a near future, a lonely writer develops an unlikely relationship with an operating system designed to meet his every need. A thoughtful exploration of love, loneliness, and human connection in the digital age. Stars Joaquin Phoenix and Scarlett Johansson. Genre: Sci-Fi, Drama, Romance.

Tenet (2020) - Directed by Christopher Nolan. Armed with only one word - Tenet - and fighting for the survival of the entire world, a protagonist journeys through a twilight world of international espionage on a mission that will unfold in something beyond real time. Explores entropy and time inversion. Stars John David Washington. Genre: Sci-Fi, Action, Thriller.

Edge of Tomorrow (2014) - Directed by Doug Liman. A soldier fighting aliens gets to relive the same day over and over again, learning from each experience to become a better fighter. A thrilling sci-fi action film with a time-loop twist. Stars Tom Cruise and Emily Blunt. Genre: Sci-Fi, Action.

Moon (2009) - Directed by Duncan Jones. Astronaut Sam Bell has a quintessentially personal encounter toward the end of his three-year stint on the Moon, where he, alongside his computer companion GERTY, harvests a resource that has helped diminish Earth's energy problems. Stars Sam Rockwell. Genre: Sci-Fi, Drama, Mystery.
